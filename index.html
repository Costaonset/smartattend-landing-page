01 • THE PROBLEM

Attendance should not slow down learning.

Our group identified manual student attendance management as a genuine institutional problem that can be improved through software.

Where it occurs

Classrooms and lecture rooms where lecturers record student attendance.

Who is affected

Students, lecturers, class representatives and school administrators.

What happens now

Attendance may be recorded using paper registers or by calling student names.

Why it matters

The process can waste teaching time, cause errors, lose records and make attendance percentages difficult to manage.

02 • OUR SOLUTION

Meet SmartAttend.

SmartAttend is a proposed digital attendance management system that replaces a time-consuming manual process with a faster and more organized approach.

  • ✓ Digital attendance recording
  • ✓ Automatic attendance reports
  • ✓ Secure student and staff login
  • ✓ Controls against proxy attendance

Intended users

Students check their attendance, lecturers record it and view reports, class representatives assist with verification, and administrators manage classes and records.

06 • RISKS & MITIGATION

Designed with real-world risks in mind.

!

Proxy Attendance

A present student could photograph or screenshot an attendance QR code and send it to an absent friend.

Mitigation: Time-limited QR codes, unique login, location verification and lecturer review.
🔒

Unauthorized Access

Someone could gain access to another student's attendance information.

Mitigation: Strong passwords, role-based access and restricted information.
