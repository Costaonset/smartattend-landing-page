01 • The Problem

Attendance should not slow down learning.

Our group identified manual student attendance management as a genuine institutional problem that can be improved through software.

⌖

Where it occurs

Classrooms and lecture rooms where lecturers record student attendance.

◉

Who is affected

Students, lecturers, class representatives and school administrators.

✎

What happens now

Attendance may be recorded using paper registers or by calling student names.

!

Why it matters

The process can waste teaching time, cause errors, lose records and make attendance percentages difficult to manage.

02 • Our Solution

Meet SmartAttend.

SmartAttend is a proposed digital attendance management system that replaces a time-consuming manual process with a faster and more organized approach.

  • Digital attendance recording
  • Automatic attendance reports
  • Secure student and staff login
  • Controls against proxy attendance

Intended users

Students check their attendance, lecturers record it and view reports, class representatives assist with verification, and administrators manage classes and records.

03 • Features & Benefits

Simple tools. Meaningful improvement.

Everything lecturers need to take and report attendance, and everything students need to check their own.

For lecturers & students

Digital Attendance

Lecturers open a session and students check in by scanning the lecture's QR code. No paper register, no calling out names.

  • Chanda M.Present
  • Banda K.Late
  • Phiri T.Absent

Take attendance in under a minute instead of a full roll call.

For lecturers & admins

Attendance Reports

See every student's and every class's attendance percentage, calculated automatically and ready to view at any time.

  • Chanda M.94%
  • Banda K.88%
  • Phiri T.68%

Spot students falling below the required attendance early.

For everyone

Secure Login

Every student, lecturer, class representative and administrator has their own account and sees only what their role allows.

  • StudentOwn records
  • LecturerTheir classes
  • AdministratorAll classes

Students see only their own records; admins control the rest.

  • < 1 minto check in a class of 60, versus about 8 minutes of roll call*
  • 0paper registers to lose, damage or retype
  • 100%of attendance percentages calculated automatically
  • 4 roleseach seeing only the records they need

*Estimates for our proposed system, to be confirmed during testing.

06 • Risks & Mitigation

Designed with real-world risks in mind.

!

Proxy Attendance

A present student could photograph or screenshot an attendance QR code and send it to an absent friend.

Mitigation: Time-limited QR codes, unique login, location verification and lecturer review.
⚿

Unauthorized Access

Someone could gain access to another student's attendance information.

Mitigation: Strong passwords, role-based access and restricted information.
