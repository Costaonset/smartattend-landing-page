ABOUT EDU NAV

Making school information easy to find and understand.

EduNav is a centralized school information platform designed to support families, students, and educators across Zambia.

Our mission

To make school discovery clear, organized, and accessible for people looking for the right education option in their area.

Our focus

We map schools across Zambia by province, district, and town so users can narrow their search quickly and confidently.

Who it helps

Parents, students, teachers, and school administrators looking for better ways to discover and compare educational institutions.
